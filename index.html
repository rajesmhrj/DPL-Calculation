How the ledger works

  1. Snap the final scoreboard

    Screenshot the last screen of your Marriage point calculator and upload it. The Total Points row is read on your phone.

  2. Names match themselves

    "Raje.", "SARG" and even "Bhuj." land on the right player. If a name is new, pick it once and it's remembered for everyone.

  3. See who pays whom

    Games pile up until someone settles. The ledger shows the fewest payments that square everyone up.

Marriage in a minute

Three decks, 21 cards each. Draw, discard, and build your hand into runs of the same suit and sets of identical cards.

Lay down three of them and you're seen: you get to look at the joker card, the tiplu. The tiplu and the cards beside it are maal, and maal is money.

First to turn all 21 cards into sets wins the hand. Everyone settles maal with everyone else, and anyone still unseen pays extra.
